Process

Five steps, no surprises

The structure is agreed before any design work starts, so you review logic first and visuals second.

  1. Brief

    You send your notes, data, template and deadline. I confirm scope, page count and a fixed quote.

  2. Outline

    A slide-by-slide outline that maps your project to the QC Story / PDCA steps. You approve it.

  3. Draft

    Full design with native charts built from your data, on your template or a clean house style.

  4. QC

    A separate review pass checks numbers, labels, consistency and layout before you see it.

  5. Delivery

    Editable .pptx, PDF and the chart workbook. Two revision rounds are included.

FAQ

Common questions

How fast can you deliver?

Turnaround depends on page count and how ready your content is; I confirm a delivery date with every quote. Rush delivery is available: 48 hours (+30%) or 24 hours (+60%).

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions are included. Further rounds are billed at the hourly rate ($45/hour), and I tell you before any extra cost starts.

Will you sign an NDA? How do you handle confidential files?

I can review your NDA before you send any files, and we agree the confidentiality terms before work starts. I do not publish client work without written permission; every sample on this site uses invented data. I use an AI-assisted workflow with a separate quality-review pass — if your organization restricts AI tools or requires de-identified data, tell me and we will agree how files are handled before work starts. Please remove patient identifiers before sending any data.

What file formats do I get?

An editable PowerPoint file (.pptx) with native charts you can update, plus a PDF. Where charts are built from your data, the supporting Excel workbook is included. I can work on your organization's own template.

Do you write the clinical content?

No. I structure and design the quality-improvement story — the QC Story steps, analysis tools and data presentation — from the content and data your team provides. I do not provide clinical advice or create clinical evidence.

What time zone are you in?

China (UTC+8). Working from UTC+8 extends the production day for US and UK teams. I aim to reply to email within one working day.

How does payment work?

Payment method agreed in advance; invoice details are confirmed in your quote. The minimum order is $80, and the 3-page trial is free with no obligation.

What is in the free 3-page trial?

Send me your draft or notes and I redesign three pages of your choice, so you can judge quality and fit before ordering anything.
